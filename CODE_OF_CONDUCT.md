# Code of Conduct

We want JobEscape to be a friendly place to learn and build, whatever your background or experience.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, give and accept feedback kindly, assume good intent, and focus on the work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, or other behaviour that would make a reasonable person uncomfortable.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where people represent the project.

If you see a problem, report it privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**) or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and restrict participation, to keep the project welcoming.
